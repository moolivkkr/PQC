KEY MESSAGE: Hosts, servers and clients are all under our control; the risk is partial changes that leave one leg or one tool classical. • Hosts: DEFAULT:PQ through SSM turns PQ on for every OpenSSL program on an AL2023 host. That’s the point and the risk: roll it out by host group and watch for failures. • Debian’s GnuTLS has no ML-KEM, so wget and git stay classical even on a PQ-by-default OS. • Web servers and proxies: set the group list on both the listener and the upstream leg. Envoy before 1.40 is classical unless configured. • Clients: browsers and Apple 26 are PQ. Our CI images are the clients we control; use OpenSSL-linked tools. • Certificates: ML-DSA is issued by Private CA, KMS and Roles Anywhere. Serving it alone breaks Go, Java, OpenSSL 3.0 and default AL2023 clients, so it’s dual certificates on internal mTLS only. Details on the certificate slides. • Envoy 1.40, nginx and the GnuTLS findings were tested; the rest is documented.
